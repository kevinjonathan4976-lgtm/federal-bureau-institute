Information Sharing & Collaboration Unit

Published: June 2026


The Information Sharing & Collaboration Unit promotes secure communication and data exchange between departments and partner organizations.


This unit ensures that critical information is shared efficiently while maintaining strict security and privacy standards.


Collaboration systems are designed to support teamwork, intelligence sharing, and coordinated responses to global challenges.


Key Functions

  • Secure Data Exchange
  • Interagency Communication
  • Collaboration Platforms
  • Information Verification
  • Data Protection Protocols
  • Operational Coordination
