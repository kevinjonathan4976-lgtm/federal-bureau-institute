Intelligence Studies Program

Published: June 2026


The Intelligence Studies Program is designed to develop analytical skills for understanding global security environments and intelligence operations.


Students are trained in data interpretation, strategic thinking, and intelligence gathering methodologies.


The program prepares individuals for roles in analysis, research, and security planning across multiple sectors.


Learning Areas

  • Strategic Intelligence
  • Data Interpretation
  • Geopolitical Analysis
  • Risk Assessment
  • Information Processing
  • Critical Thinking Development
