Threats Intelligence

Published: June 2026


The Federal Bureau Institute Threat Intelligence Division is dedicated to monitoring, analyzing, and responding to emerging global security threats across digital and physical environments.


Our analysts work continuously to identify cyber threats, organized criminal networks, and potential risks that may impact critical infrastructure and public safety systems.


Using advanced data analytics, artificial intelligence, and real-time monitoring systems, the division ensures rapid detection and response to suspicious activities.


Collaboration with international agencies and research institutions strengthens our ability to prevent and mitigate complex security threats.


Core Focus Areas

  • Cyber Threat Detection
  • Risk Assessment Analysis
  • Network Security Monitoring
  • Digital Forensics
  • Global Intelligence Sharing
  • Incident Response Coordination
