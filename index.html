About Federal Bureau Institute

The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) is the premier federal law enforcement and domestic intelligence agency of the United States. Established in 1908, the FBI is responsible for protecting the American people and upholding the Constitution of the United States. The agency investigates federal crimes, combats terrorism, cybercrime, public corruption, organized crime, and threats to national security. Through intelligence gathering, advanced technology, and partnerships with local, state, federal, and international agencies, the FBI works to safeguard communities and defend the nation against evolving threats.
