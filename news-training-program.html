Training Program Applications Now Open

Published: June 2026


The Federal Bureau Institute is pleased to announce that applications are now open for its annual Technology and Innovation Training Program.


The program is designed for students, researchers, technology professionals, and individuals seeking to improve their knowledge of cybersecurity, digital intelligence, emerging technologies, and leadership development.


Participants will gain access to expert-led workshops, practical training sessions, research seminars, and collaborative innovation projects designed to enhance professional skills and industry knowledge.


The Institute's goal is to develop future leaders capable of driving innovation, solving complex challenges, and contributing positively to the global technology ecosystem.


Program Benefits

  • Technology Workshops
  • Research Projects
  • Leadership Development
  • Professional Networking
  • Innovation Challenges
  • Certificate of Participation
